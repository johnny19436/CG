Overview
● Slerp (Spherical Linear Interpolation)
slerp(q₁, q₂, u) = (sin(1 − u)Ω / sin Ω) q₁ + (sin uΩ / sin Ω) q₂

Overview
● Squad (Spherical Quadrangle Interpolation)
○ Bezier Interpolation of Quaternions
○ provide Higher-Order Continuity than Slerp
Squad(p, a, b, q; α) = Slerp(Slerp(p, q; α), slerp(a, b; α); 2(1 − α)α)
source: https://splines.readthedocs.io/en/latest/rotation/squad.html
